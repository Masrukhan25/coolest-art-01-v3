# The Lantern That Became the Milky Way

## What the picture shows
One small child stands on a dark cliff edge, seen from behind, holding a paper lantern up with both hands. Golden light pours out of the lantern and rises as a river of stars that becomes the Milky Way, one warm light against a deep blue night.

## What it means
One small light can fill the whole sky. The night is not empty — it is waiting to be lit.
